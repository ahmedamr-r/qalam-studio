# Qalam Studio v0.1: the cut

**Who it's for:** a founder or PM on their own, or a team of two or three, with a pile of material about their product and customers, and no time to read it twice.

**What they get in the first ten minutes:** what they have turned into problems ranked by what backs them, each line resting on a source's own words, found word for word, saying what kind of evidence they are.

That's the whole pitch. Everything below either serves it or is hidden.

## The loop

1. **Open Qalam.** It opens on the person's organizations (the first time, the welcome: it finds Claude Code or Codex, and the person names the project).
2. **Add what you have.** In Qalam AI's chat beside the canvas: paste it or drop files (PDF, Word, Markdown, CSV or XLSX), or put files in the inbox folder. Customers' words are one option among several: Qalam AI says what each document is and marks each point (people said, measured, studied, read elsewhere, our thinking), and the person can change it.
3. **Problems.** One page per need (We know · We believe · Against · Open questions), every line citing points found in their source, ranked beside the canvas by sources, then points, then the newest. People said, measured and studied reach "We know"; read elsewhere and our thinking stay in "We believe".
4. **Ask.** A question in the chat, about everything added, answered with quotes that are checked.
5. **Goals.** Write what you're trying to move on the canvas, or let Qalam AI find one with a few questions in the chat. Qalam links the problems that bear on each goal, and a goal's page lists them, strongest first.
6. **Next.** On a problem's page, Draft solutions (each with its riskiest assumptions and one test), then work them on the canvas.

Everything happens on one canvas per space: the organization's, and each product's if it has any. Qalam AI's chat sits on its left, as Figma's panel does, and takes whatever's brought to it: a question, their thinking (a brain dump, placed as cards), any document, a goal or a problem. A goal's or a problem's page opens in the panel on the right; there are no Goals, Problems or Evidence pages.

Step 3 is where the value shows. A first run that doesn't reach it in 10 minutes has failed.

## What's hidden, and why

**A second product inside.** Screens, design systems, prototypes and Design this are a design tool for a different person with a different promise. `src/codegen` and `src/design-system` alone are about 30k lines. Nobody who comes for discovery expects them, and they double what a newcomer has to make sense of. Hide these first.

**Teams.** The Yours step, the Decisions place and the why prompt. v0.1 is for one person, in a company team or on their own. Decisions keep being recorded quietly; only the page and the prompt are hidden. Products stay (changed October 31): they're optional, and nobody is asked to sort goals into them.

**Anything that needs a connector or another service.** Every connector in the catalog, feeds, numbers from PostHog, product code from GitHub, Figma pictures at funnel steps, and work status from Linear, Jira and GitHub. Each needs a sign-in, and several are written from docs and have never run against the real service. Files and paste cover a first user. Bring back the one connector the first five users ask for.

**The goal page's extras.** Jobs and gaps (Unknowns), What others aim for, Break it down, the funnel and its step pins, Flows toward it, and on the canvas, Draft its problems. They make a goal's page long before it has a single problem on it.

**Reading beyond what they bring.** People online (Look online, posts jobs), What people see (screenshots read by `qalam-see`) and Review now. Reddit refuses reads, the screenshot reader needs a native helper, and people online blurs the one rule v0.1 sells: a line says what it rests on. Needs a look keeps code's checks, which cost no tokens.

**Extra ways in.** The first run's Voice step and quick capture from the menu bar (⌃⌥Space). A step that asks about a microphone and a global hotkey are two more things that can go wrong on a stranger's Mac. The mic in the boxes stays (talking is quicker than typing out what customers said), and so does the inbox folder.

**Two of the setup's doors.** "Help me find them" (a brain dump with rounds of questions) and the idea door, plus the You step. v0.1 is for people who already have material. The idea door serves a different person (no customers yet), and everything it makes lands in "We believe".

**Questions before a draft.** On a card nothing backs, its next steps can ask what you know first and keep your answers as your own words. Like the idea door, everything that makes lands in "We believe". Without it, a bare problem offers Find what backs it first, and Draft solutions drafts at once.

**Five engines.** OpenAI, Gemini and OpenRouter keys, Ollama and LM Studio. Their web search is written from the providers' docs and hasn't been checked against a real key, and a model on the person's computer makes quality depend on a model you haven't tested. Claude Code, Codex (checked against 0.155) and an Anthropic key stay.

**Draft its tests.** Draft solutions already gives each solution its riskiest assumptions and one test, so a second button that does the same thing only confuses.

**Windows.** The installed app has never run there. Take the `windows` job out of the release for v0.1; typecheck and tests on Windows stay in `checks.yml`.

## How to hide it

One switch, not a branch, so nothing is deleted and an organization made with everything on still opens. Built on October 31, 2026.

- `src/lab.ts` names each hidden feature (`design`, `decisions`, `you`, `yours`, `voice`, `doors`, `questions`, `engines`, `connectors`, `code`, `feeds`, `goals`, `online`, `seen`, `review`, `tests`, `capture`) and exports `lab(feature)`. Everything is off by default.
- To turn it on, set `"labs": true` in `~/Qalam/.qalam/settings.json` for everything, or a list such as `"labs": ["feeds", "voice"]` to bring back one feature at a time. `QALAM_LAB=1` (or `QALAM_LAB=feeds,voice`) wins over the setting. A reload picks it up; the desktop app's quick capture needs a restart.
- Each entry point below checks it. Routes, server code and the organization's files stay as they are.
- Work that calls out without being asked stops when its feature is off: feeds aren't loaded or checked as the canvas opens. Bearings after filing stay, since Goals need them.
- Tests run with everything on (`vitest.setup.ts`). `src/lab.test.ts` and the first run's tests turn it off and check that places, first-run steps, engines and connectors leave the hidden ones out.

## How you'll know it worked

Find five people who fit the first line of this page, with different material between them: not only interviews, but reports, research, a strategy doc. In a 30-minute call with each, watch them use Qalam on their own material. Put your notes from each call into Qalam.

- 3 of the 5 reach ranked problems from their own material in the first session.
- 2 of the 5 open it again within a week without being asked.
- At least one says a problem's page showed them something they hadn't seen.

If those hold, turn the hidden features back on one at a time, starting with what the five asked for. If they don't, fix the loop rather than adding features.

## Decided: one canvas

The home was the canvas, with Goals, Problems and Evidence as pages beside it. On October 31 those pages went: the canvas is the only place, and its panel ranks the problems (each opening its page in the panel), so someone with ten interviews and no goals sees them on the first screen. Watch the five: if they never touch the canvas itself and only read the panel, give the ranked problems more of the screen in v0.2.
