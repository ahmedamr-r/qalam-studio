Install Qalam Studio

  1. 1Drag Qalam Studio into Applications.
  2. 2Open it. The first time, macOS stops it: click Done.
  3. 3In System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway and confirm.

On macOS 13 or 14, Control-click Qalam Studio in Applications and choose Open.
The full guide: docs/install-mac.md on github.com/ahmedamr-r/qalam-studio
