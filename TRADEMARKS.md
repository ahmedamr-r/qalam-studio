## What this means for forks

Forks are welcome. To keep it clear which build people are using:

- If you share a build of your fork (hosted, packaged or downloadable), give it a different name and a different logo.
- You may say your fork is "based on Qalam Studio", and link to this repository.
- You don't need to rename anything to run Qalam Studio for yourself or your team without changes, to try it, to study it, or to send changes back to this project.
- Don't suggest that the official project made or endorses your fork unless that's true.

If you're not sure whether your use is fine, open an issue and ask.
