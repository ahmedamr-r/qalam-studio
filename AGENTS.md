# Qalam Studio: notes for coding agents

- **UI:** a React single-page app built with Vite. `index.html` loads `src/main.tsx`, and `src/ui/App.tsx` picks the page from the address (`src/ui/routes.ts`). Moving between the frame's pages (a space's canvas, Decisions, Connections) happens in place (`src/ui/nav.ts`: links, ⌘K and `navigate` push the address, Back and Forward work, under one Suspense boundary in a transition, so there's no splash); a canvas stays mounted while only its query changes (`App.tsx` keys it on the path). Everything else (the organizations' list, a Screens project, setup, the welcome, another organization) is a full page load: a project's document and everything module-level belong to one load. A board has no page of its own any more: its cards are worked on on its space's home (see The space's canvas), and its old address (`/o/<org>/d/<id>`) lands there with `?board=<id>` and the rest of its query (`boardHomeHref`, `src/ui/space/href.ts`).
  - **Qalam opens on the projects** on this computer (`/`, `src/ui/org/OrgsPage.tsx`): the one box that starts a project first (`StartBox`, as the first run has it), then the projects, the one opened last first, and Open a folder. The Qalam mark and the project menu's All projects go back to it (`orgsHref`). With none yet, the welcome.
  - **People read "project" for an organization** everywhere they read it (the top bar, menus, messages, `wordsFor`), so it's one thing they work on; the code, its files and Qalam AI's prompts still say organization (`org`). A team's project still says the team and its Company.
  - Every page sits in `PageFrame` (`src/ui/frame/`). It provides the top bar, toasts, dialogs, and a page ⌘K (`PagePalette`, keys in `src/commands/pages.ts`; ↵ goes to what it found, and asks the org first only for a question, `withAsk`). There's no sidebar: the left of a canvas is Qalam AI's chat (see The space's canvas). The top bar holds the organization's menu (switch, make or rename one, About, New product, Decisions where it's on, `OrgSwitcher`), then the canvas's menu once there are products (`CanvasMenu`, `src/ui/frame/CanvasMenu.tsx`: the product's name, or All products on the organization's own canvas; it goes to another canvas, makes a product, says what this one is about), where you are (a page beside the canvas leads back to it, "Canvas"), the page's own actions, then Connections (with the health dot) and the theme (`FrameTools`).
  - There are no Goals, Problems or Evidence pages: everything is worked on on the canvas. The places are the canvas (`home`, the space's own address), Decisions and Connections, `/o/<org>/decisions|connections` (`src/ui/frame/places.ts`, `placeHref`). An old place's address (`goals`, `problems`, `evidence`, and `boards`, `topics`, `sources` from before; `GONE_PLACES`) lands on its space's canvas (`movedTo`), and so does the home's old `?tab=`; `evidence?add=1` opens adding there.
  - **Spaces** (`src/ui/frame/spaces.ts`, pure): the organization's own canvas, named after it, which shows all of it (each thing marked with its product), or one product's, which shows only that product's (`inSpace`). A product's is at `/o/<org>/products/<id>` (`spaceHref`; `/p/` is a Screens board); Connections is the organization's in every space. Products are in every version and optional: an organization works on its own canvas until it has some. Opening an organization (`openOrgHref`, `/o/<org>/products`) lands on the canvas this tab was on last there, else its own (App's boot). The top bar reads Basket / Grocery app (the canvas's menu).
    - Pages get the space from the address (`useSpace`, a product that's gone goes to the organization's); `lastSpace` (session storage) is the tab's last, and `currentSpace` the one the window is on. Links from outside a space page use `hereHref`.
    - The old sidebar's `?product=` filter redirects to the space (`movedTo`; `?product=org` to the organization's).
    - The canvas's panel (see The space's canvas) is closed until something asks for it, so the canvas has the room. With nothing picked it opens from the buttons at the canvas's top right (`SectionButtons`, one section at a time, again or × closes it; `?panel=problems` opens it from an address): the space's problems ranked by what backs them (`SpaceProblems`, `ProblemsList`: filtered by goal, New problem, each opening its page in the panel), Needs a look grouped by goal (a notice picks its card on the canvas), with goals that feed no goal yet above it (their count is on the button), the segment and product pages (`SpacePages`), and the lab's parts under More (`LabSections`: design work, what people see, product maps). A card picked takes the panel over and letting it go gives it back (closed, or the section that was open), and the card comes back into view if the panel cut it off (`keepInView`, once the press is let go); a goal's or a problem's page opens in it.
  - People see one word per thing: Goal (an outcome; Sub-goal), Problem (an opportunity, or a topic page about a need), Solution, Test, Board (a Discovery project). Type names in code stay as they were (`KIND_LABEL` in `src/discovery/kinds.ts`, `TOPIC_KIND_LABEL`). A board is storage: each goal's tree is kept in its own board's file, and people never open a board as such.
  - **The Desk** (`globals.css`, section 8): a page's shell carries a soft gradient in one hue, indigo (`q-shell`; its top bar `r.shellBar`), and its panels lie on it (an 8px gutter, `rounded-xl`, `shadow-panel`). A space's canvas carries a faint echo (`q-canvas-wash`, `--canvas-wash`). A board's canvas shows it by clearing to transparent (`Renderer.setSeeThrough`, set in `CanvasView`), and `--color-canvas` stays solid because the painter picks its palette from that colour's lightness (`readChromeTheme`). Cards, buttons, tags and text take no gradient, and a Screens project stays flat, for judging a design on. Panels and what floats on the canvas are glass (`q-glass`: a tint and a backdrop blur over their `bg-*`, which stays for less transparency); the canvas and a panel's composer, fields and menus stay solid, and a question card or a row in the chat is a quiet sheet on it (`q-sheet`). A backdrop filter traps `position: fixed` inside it, so anything fixed that a panel opens goes through a portal. `shell-tokens.test.ts` holds the contrast. A card on a canvas is solid, an indigo slate on dark and white on light, with 12px corners and hairlines and wires in the same hue, and its tags are neutral with the colour only on their marks (`--color-card*`, `--color-wire`, `CARD_LOOK`, `TAG`). The Qalam mark is a q in one stroke, its bowl a card and its tail a wire, and Qalam AI's glyph a pair of nuqtas (`src/ui/brand/geometry.ts`). CSS transitions stay off until `armMotion` lifts the boot gate (`App.tsx`, or a Screens project's studio), so a new kind of top-level page must go through it.
  - **One look.** A board paints its cards and sources with CanvasKit (`src/engine/ost-painter.ts`, `lane-painter.ts`), taking sizes, kind marks, tag, footer, source tile and wire geometry from `src/discovery/look.ts`, and colours from theme tokens (`--color-kind-*`, `--color-card*`, `--color-wire`) that `src/engine/ost-palette.ts` repeats; `ost-palette.test.ts` fails when the two differ. Change the look there.
  - Each card wears its kind as a tag on its top edge, half above it: a neutral chip with the kind's mark in its own colour. A board card's footer, under a rule, holds the chips and then the stage; linked work and a notice sit at the top edge's right end (`cardBox` in `src/discovery/metrics.ts`).
  - A Screens project's rail shows the space's canvas and Connections above its own panels (`RailPlaces`). They save before leaving (`leaveTo`).
- **Server:** `server/` is a small Hono app (`server/app.ts`). Each route in `server/routes/*.ts` takes a standard `Request` and returns a `Response`; AI routes stream NDJSON. Model calls happen only there.
  - `pnpm dev` runs Vite on 127.0.0.1:3101 with the server mounted at `/api/*` (`server/vite.ts`). An edit to a route or to `src/ai` takes effect on the next request.
  - `pnpm build && pnpm start` serves `dist/` and the server from one Node process (`server/serve.ts`).
  - Requests are checked by `server/guard.ts` (Host must be this machine; writes must come from this origin; optional launch token).
- **Desktop app:** `electron/main.ts` serves the built UI at `qalam://app` and forwards `/api/*` to the core, a background utility process (`server/core.ts`), over a message port (`server/rpc.ts`). No network port is opened, except the one-time sign-in listener on 127.0.0.1 (see Connectors). `vite.desktop.config.ts` bundles both into `out/`, so the installed app needs only Electron.
  - `pnpm desktop` builds and opens it; `pnpm desktop:dev` opens the window on the Vite dev server.
  - `bin/qalam-studio.mjs` is the `npx qalam-studio` launcher: it runs the Electron npm installed on the package's `out/` and `dist/`. Electron's package fetches its binary at first use with the running Node and needs 22.12 or newer, so the launcher checks that first and says what to do (`engines` says the same). The launcher runs that fetch itself (`bin/fetch-electron.mjs`), since Electron's shows no progress and has no timeout and one bad connection to GitHub crawled at 20 KB/s where a new one got 3 MB/s: it says how much has come, and a connection that brings under 1 MB in 45 s is dropped for a new one, up to twice. `prepack` builds, then `scripts/check-pack.mjs` stops a package missing the Mac helpers, since the build goes on without them. `node-llama-cpp` is an optional dependency: npm drops it where it can't run (an Intel Node on an Apple silicon Mac), search goes by words, and health says "Not on this install" (`missingRunner`, status `absent`), told apart by what the worker hit.
  - **The menu bar (`electron/tray.ts`):** Qalam stays there when its window closes (`desktop.json`'s `keepInMenuBar`), so the inbox and connectors keep working.
    - Its menu (`trayMenuTemplate`, pure) shows whether Qalam AI is ready, from `/api/health` through `coreFetch`, plus opening at login.
    - ⌃⌥Space opens quick capture: a small window on `/capture` that posts a note, quote or link to `POST /api/org/:org/quick` (`server/quick.ts`). That writes a file into the inbox, which takes it from there.
  - The page's security policy is in `electron/assets.ts`. It allows `unsafe-eval` only because the prototype runner evaluates compiled code (`src/ui/prototype/runtime.ts`).
  - `pnpm dist:mac` packages the Mac app (Apple silicon): `scripts/stage-app.mjs` puts what the installed app loads in `release/app` (out/, dist/, and node-llama-cpp installed by npm, since this package's own `package.json` lists Electron for npx), then electron-builder runs with that folder as its project (`electron-builder.yml`, paths relative to it; the entitlements are found in `build/` by their default names, since a path set there reaches codesign unresolved). The models process and Ask's bridge run the app's binary as Node (`ELECTRON_RUN_AS_NODE`), so never turn that fuse off. The installed app takes the login shell's PATH (`loginShellPath`, `server/claude.ts`) and updates itself from GitHub Releases (`electron/updates.ts`). `.github/workflows/release.yml` builds a tag into a draft release that starts with how to install it (`build/release-notes.md`), signed and notarized with the Apple secrets (`docs/releasing.md`).
    - Without them (no paid Apple account yet, October 2026) the Mac app is signed ad hoc (`pnpm dist:mac:adhoc`, `-c.mac.identity=-`; with no signature at all a downloaded copy reads as damaged). macOS stops it once and people open it with Open Anyway (`docs/install-mac.md`). Its `.dmg` window shows those steps: `build/dmg-open-anyway.html`, rendered to the `.tiff` beside it by `scripts/render-dmg.mjs` in a hidden window, since offscreen rendering fails with Electron 45 on macOS 26.
    - An ad-hoc app can't install an update, so it says a version is out and opens its page (`signingTeam` in `electron/signature.ts`).
    - `homebrew.yml` writes the cask into the tap when a notarized release is published; Homebrew refuses one signed ad hoc.
  - **Windows** (x64): `pnpm dist:win` packages an NSIS installer (unsigned for now; `release.yml`'s `windows` job puts it in the same draft), and `.github/workflows/checks.yml` runs typecheck and tests on macOS and Windows for each pull request. Typecheck and tests pass on Windows in CI (October 30, 2026); the installed app itself hasn't been run on Windows yet.
    - Paths inside an organization are written with `/` on every system (the store's `rel`, the folder watcher's names), and Windows' own system folders can't be an organization.
    - A write renames a temp file over the real one; Windows refuses while anything holds that file open for a moment, so `renameOver` (`server/org/fsx.ts`) tries again for about two seconds.
    - Claude Code and Codex are found as `claude.exe`/`codex.exe` (native installer, `~\.local\bin`) or npm's `.cmd` in `%APPDATA%\npm` (`findOnWindows`). Every start goes through `spawnCli`/`execCli` (`src/ai/commands.ts`): a `.cmd` shim's script runs with Node (or the app's own binary as Node), never through cmd.exe, and a system prompt goes in a file (`--system-prompt-file`, `promptArgs`) since Windows caps a command line at 32,767 characters.
    - Secrets: the main process keeps them sealed with `safeStorage` in `userData/secrets.json` (`electron/secrets.ts`); the core asks over its parent port (`QALAM_SECRETS=electron`, `useSealedSecrets`). A sign-in page opens with `rundll32 url.dll,FileProtocolHandler`. Windows gives an app the user's PATH, so there's no login shell to ask. Voice and reading words on screenshots stay the Mac's.
    - The Connections section is "This computer"; copy says "this computer" except where a feature is the Mac's own.
- **Organizations and products:** pages live under their organization: `/o/<org>` is its canvas (`src/ui/home/Home.tsx` with `src/ui/org/`), `/o/<org>/d/<id>` a board's old address; `/` lists the organizations, and addresses from before (`/d/<id>`) find the organization and move there (`src/ui/App.tsx`). Build links with `projectHref`/`orgHref` (`src/projects/model.ts`), never by hand. A board (`Project.product`) and a source (`SourceRecord.product`) are for one product or the whole organization; products are `products/<slug>.md` and the organization's own context `about.md` (`src/org/context-md.ts`), and Qalam AI reads the board's with `contextFor` (`src/org/context.ts`). Sources belong to the organization and list the boards that use them (`boards`); taking one off a board keeps it. A card's quote names its piece (`OstEvidence.piece`, `hub:quote:<id>`).
- **Storage:** an organization is a folder in `~/Qalam` (`QALAM_HOME` moves it; tests and browser checks must point it at a scratch folder). `server/org/` owns it: `codecs.ts` turns projects and sources into readable files, `store.ts` keeps the index, versions (409 on a stale document save) and the folder watcher, `routes.ts` serves `/api/orgs` and `/api/org/:org/*`. The browser side is `src/org/` (client, startup gate, live changes, conflict choice, import from browser storage); the repos in `src/projects/db.ts`, `src/discovery/db.ts`, `src/knowledge/db.ts` and `src/ui/ai/chatDb.ts` go through it.
- **Goals:** one tree per organization in `goals.md` (`src/goals/`, codec `goals-md.ts`): the North Star under its heading with each goal nested under the goal it feeds, then "Not under a goal yet". It's the only place a goal lives; the About and product files no longer hold goals (`legacyGoals` moves an old "## Goals" section in once, `migrateGoals` at store open, ids fixed so two computers write the same file).
  - A board's outcome card is its goal on the canvas: the goal says which card (`on: {board, card}`), and the card keeps a copy of its words, number and stage to paint. While the board is open, `src/ui/discovery/goals.ts` brings the two together (`reconcile` in `src/goals/mirror.ts`): a goal's change elsewhere reaches the card outside undo (and the undo history keeps it), an edit on the board goes to the goal. A new outcome card gets its goal (one already named in its words first). The server never writes a board for a goal.
  - Goal decisions (target, a number moved by hand, status, the goal it feeds, the North Star) are found by comparing the tree before and after an API write (`goalDecisions`, `src/goals/decisions.ts`), recorded under the board "goals", undone when taken back within 10 minutes; a hand edit of the file records none. The board's recorder skips a linked card's number and stage (`DiffContext.linked`).
  - Qalam AI hears the goals through `goalsText` (`src/goals/text.ts`) wherever it hears the About; goals are `context` pieces with sub `goal` (`hub:goal:<id>`), under their parent and linked to their card (`for`). Which goals are this computer's person's: `mine` in `.qalam/local`.
  - **A goal's page** opens in the canvas's panel (`?goal=<id>`, `GoalView` in `src/ui/goals/GoalPage.tsx`, data from `goalPage` in `server/org/goals.ts`; see The space's canvas): its number, the goal it feeds and the goals under it, where it's worked on, the problems behind it and its decisions. "Work on it" puts the problem under the goal's card on this canvas once (`addProblem`); a goal with no board has its own card there (its id), and the save starts its board.
  - **Which problems bear on which goals** (`hub/bearings.jsonl`, folded by `foldBearings`): one call (`bearingsCall`, `src/ai/goals.ts`) reads need pages against goals; `sanitizeBearings` keeps a link only when its ids were sent, it cites its problem's own lines and its words and numbers are in them (or the goal's number). A long page sends the lines search ranks nearest the goal (`nearGoals`). "Find problems" runs it for a goal and the goals under it (replacing Qalam AI's earlier links there); `startBearings` runs it after Qalam AI files material into need pages (topic events with `filed`), for those pages against every active goal. A person's link or unlink stands: Qalam AI never makes a link they took off. Each call's trace goes in `chats/goals/traces`. `rankForGoal` (`src/goals/problems.ts`) orders the problems behind a goal: direct ones first, then by sources, quotes and newest; a sub-goal's show through it.
  - Needs a look: a goal's number not updated in weeks is checked on the goal (`runChecks` with `goals`), and each notice carries the goals it bears on (`withGoals` in `server/org/notices.ts`).
  - **What we don't know yet:** `gapsOf` (`src/goals/gaps.ts`, pure, no tokens) finds a goal's gaps: no number, where it stands, no target, no problems, a problem only the team raises, a problem no segment page cites, its pages' open questions. Each gap's key stays the same while it's open.
    - **Jobs** close them (`src/goals/jobs.ts`, `hub/jobs.jsonl`: a job's line, then a line per change, folded by `foldJobs`). One call (`jobsCall`, `src/ai/jobs.ts`) plans them from the goal, its gaps and problems, the tools here and whether each is connected (`jobTools`), and the segment pages and people the sources name. `sanitizeJobs` keeps a job only when its gap, tool and people were sent, its words and numbers are in what was sent, and its gap has no open job.
    - A search job runs in its tool (`searchForJob`); the items picked go through `importItems` like any material, then `flushBearings` links what was filed to the goals at once, and the job is done with what came of it (`takeInForJob`, `server/org/jobs.ts`). A numbers job reads the goal's number from PostHog (`MetricSource`, `src/ui/numbers/`). A talk job is handed over: its questions and who to ask; notes posted to `/capture` with the job's id are kept on it and linked at once (`noteForJob`). A read-code job is answered from the product's code once it's connected (see Product code). A posts job looks online for its gap's problem (`onlineForJob`, see People online).
    - The goal page lists each gap with its job (`src/ui/goals/Unknowns.tsx`); the home's goal cards show each goal's next job (`useJobs`).
  - **What others aim for and Break it down** (`server/org/outside.ts`, kept per goal in `hub/goals/<goal>.json`, `changeGoalWork` in the store):
    - What others aim for: the research step looks it up on the web (purpose `goals`, `research()` in `src/ai/server.ts`), the core reads the pages it links itself (`fetchGuarded`, `htmlText`), and one call (`claimsCall`, `src/ai/outside.ts`) picks out the numbers others report from excerpts around the numbers the notes name (`pageExcerpt`). `sanitizeClaims` (`src/goals/outside.ts`) keeps a claim only when its quote is on its page word for word and its number is in the quote. They're never sources, never customer evidence, never a goal's number.
    - Break it down: one call (`breakdownCall`, `src/ai/breakdown.ts`) reads the goal and the goals above, the levers the person picked (their answer), a pack of their material anchored on the goal and what others report, and proposes the goals under it, each on a lever. `sanitizeBreakdown` keeps a part only when its words and numbers are in what it cites or in the goals above (what others report shows beside a part, never in it), and a number only from their words. Whether the parts make the goal's number is code's arithmetic (`checkParts`, `src/goals/math.ts`: a product or a sum, a rate in % multiplying as a fraction, within 5%). Accepting puts a part in `goals.md` under the goal (`decidePart`). The goal's page and the canvas's next steps ask through one client (`src/org/goal-work.ts`); `GET /goals/:id/work` reads the goal's work file alone.
- **Decisions:** a board's decisions are found by comparing it before and after a change (`decisionsFromDiff` in `src/decisions/diff.ts`), never by instrumenting commands, so every way of changing the board is covered. `src/ui/decisions/recorder.ts` runs that 1.5 s after changes settle, sends what it found to `decisions/<YYYY-MM>.md` (`server/org/store.ts`, Markdown in `src/decisions/format.ts`), asks why on big ones (`WhyPrompt.tsx`) and marks a decision undone when it's taken back. Accepting or discarding Qalam AI's proposals goes through `recordAccepted`/`recordDiscarded`. Adding or rewording a card is not a decision.
- **Search:** the organization's knowledge is indexed as pieces (one fact each: a quote, need, card, assumption, decision, line about a product) with links between them (`src/hub/pieces.ts`). `server/index/` keeps `.qalam/index.sqlite` current from the store's change events (only what changed is written; it can always be deleted and rebuilt) and searches by words (FTS5), meaning (vectors) and links, fused by rank (`server/index/search.ts`); `packFor` fits the result into a token budget with `[k1]` ids (`src/hub/pack.ts`). The embedding model runs in its own process (`server/models/`, node-llama-cpp, downloaded on first use). Change a step only with `pnpm eval:retrieval` before and after: it scores search on a labelled sample organization (`eval/retrieval/`), and its numbers chose today's setup.
- **Calls with the organization's knowledge:** a board's AI calls (draft, extract, synthesize, dump-place) pack what bears on them on the client (`src/org/pack.ts` → `POST /api/org/:org/pack`), leaving out what the call already sends, and pass it as `context` (block, `k` ids, which ids are quotes); routes stay stateless (`withKnowledge`, `knowledgeBlock`, `withPieceReliance` in `src/ai/discovery.ts`). Quotes from other sources load as "visiting" sources (`src/ui/discovery/sources.ts`) so proposals can attach them; accepting puts the source on the board. `recordCall` (`src/ui/discovery/knowledge.ts`) keeps each call's trace: what it was given, and only the cited ids it was sent. No index (Node 22) means no context, and the calls work as before.
- **What's known about the market** (`hub/market.md`, codec `src/market/market-md.ts`; `src/market/`, `server/org/market.ts`): the company's facts, its competitors (one `###` each) and its domain ("What companies like it aim for", "What their customers struggle with"), each line with the page it was read on and its record in a comment (`by`, `url`, the page's own words in `quote`, a hash of its words: a line whose words a person changed, or one written with no record, is theirs, `by: "you"`, never rewritten). Read on the web or the team's say-so, never customers' evidence. Indexed as `context` pieces (sub `market`, `competitor`, `domain`, `marketPieces`); calls hear it through `marketText` (`src/market/text.ts`: competitors, and the domain where asked, said to be read on the web), the dump's calls on their own `market` field. The Company panel fills it (see On the canvas below): each lookup runs in the core and answers at once (`lookUpCompany`, `lookForCompetitors`, `lookUpDomain`): the research step (purposes `company`, `competitors`, `domain`), the pages read by code (`readPage`), one call (`companyCall`, `competitorsCall`, `domainCall`, kind `market`), and code keeps a line only where its quote is on its page word for word with its numbers in it (`sanitizeFacts` in `src/market/facts.ts`, `sanitizeCompetitors`, `sanitizeDomain` in `src/market/rivals.ts`). Their progress is kept in `chats/market/session.json` (`src/market/session.ts`, a `market` event with `session`), so the panel follows it and a reload picks it up (a look this process isn't running says it stopped). `GET /api/org/:org/market`, `POST`/`PUT /market/company` and `/market/competitors`; `src/org/market.ts` in the window.
  - **On the canvas:** the Company button (Project on your own; `?panel=company`, `CompanySection`, `src/ui/org/`) shows what's known on every canvas, each line with the page it was read on. A person fixes a line, takes it off or adds one (`editMarket`, `src/market/edit.ts`, pure; `PUT /api/org/:org/market/lines`, `editKnown`): a fixed line is theirs, a competitor taken off is never proposed again until they add it back, and a company fact fixed or taken off changes its sentence in the About where it's still word for word (`aboutAfterFact`). Nothing kept yet: the panel takes its website or a line about it and reads up on it there (`LookUpCompany`, `src/ui/org/CompanyLookUp.tsx`), each fact kept, fixed or dropped; competitors the same way (`LookForRivals`); the domain is read once a fact is kept.
  - **Who hears it:** `marketContext` (`src/market/text.ts`: the facts the About doesn't hold, and the competitors) reaches a board's calls through `contextFor`, the core's through `productLine`, and Ask's guide (`hub/market.md` is in its list of files). Draft solutions, Draft its problems and Draft its tests hear it through `companyContext` (`server/org/company.ts`), put on their material after its `hay` (`withCompany`), so it words a draft and no number or quote can come from it. A brain dump's calls hear the domain too (`market`).
- **The project's words** (`hub/terms.md`, codec `src/terms/terms-md.ts`; `src/terms/`, pure; `terms` in code, since `src/org/words.ts` and `src/hub/words.ts` hold other "words"; people see Words): a glossary and nothing else, each term with what it is in a sentence or two and the words the project says to avoid for it, its record in a comment (a hash of its words: a term whose words a person changed, or one written with no record, is theirs). A term taken off goes in `gone` and is never proposed again until a person adds it back; anything under a heading the codec doesn't know is kept as notes. Edited in the Company panel's Words part (`WordsGroup`, `src/ui/org/`; `PUT /api/org/:org/terms` with `editTerms`, `changeTerms` under its lock, a `terms` event; `src/org/terms.ts` in the window, loaded at boot). Indexed as `context` pieces (sub `term`, `termPieces`), so search and Ask find what a word means (Ask's guide lists the file). Every call hears it (`termsText`, those the call's words use first): `contextFor`, `companyContext` (before the market, so a long market is what's cut), the core's `productLine` and statement wording, whose names may come from it. Where something new is compared with what's there, an avoided word reads as its term (`termsFold`, `sameTopic`'s `fold`): plan mode, Draft solutions, Draft its problems, Break it down, goal ideas, goals found in a document, and filing's new pages and segments, so "Cart abandonment" isn't proposed beside "Basket abandonment".
- **What a point rests on** (`basis`, `src/discovery/basis.ts`, pure): Qalam reads any document for what it states; customers' words are one kind of material among several. Each point (a `SourceQuote`, found word for word as ever) says where its words come from: `said` (people said), `measured`, `studied`, `read` (read elsewhere) or `own` (our thinking). `reachesKnow`: said, measured and studied reach "We know" and "Against"; read and own stay in "We believe".
